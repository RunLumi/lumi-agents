# Names, branding and independent distributions

This policy concerns branding, not an additional restriction on Apache-2.0
copyright permissions. It relies only on rights that actually exist and applicable
law; it does not assert trademark registration or exclusive ownership of every
use of the word "Lumi". See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

RunLumi uses **RunLumi**, **Lumi Agents** and the associated product marks to identify
its official work. Apache-2.0 section 6 does not grant general trademark rights.

## Accurate reference is welcome

You may accurately identify the upstream project in documentation, reviews,
tutorials, citations, dependency lists and compatibility statements. For example,
"Acme Automation, based on Lumi Agents" identifies an independent product without
claiming endorsement. Preserve notices required by the relevant software licenses.
No permission request is needed for lawful descriptive or nominative use.

## Avoid confusing users

Use a distinct product name and visual identity for a modified or independently
operated distribution. Do not present it as an official RunLumi build, use RunLumi's
signing identity, imply security certification/support we did not provide, or use
confusing branding in domains, app-store listings or hosted services. Ask for a
written branding agreement before presenting an OEM/partner offering as official.
The assessment is subject to applicable law and actual likelihood of confusion.

Private source forks and development builds need not remove required notices or
perform a cosmetic rebrand merely to contribute patches. Clarify provenance before
public distribution. Accurate attribution can remain when product branding changes.

Copyright permissions for included artwork remain whatever that artwork's license
grants. This policy does not retroactively withdraw those permissions. Attribution
is not a forced advertising placement, and the policy cannot prohibit an otherwise
lawful competing implementation merely for competing.

## Stewardship

Maintainers should retain evidence of creation/use, verify ownership and relevant
registrations before making claims, and seek legal review before enforcement.
This document does not register a mark or give a partner a trademark license.
